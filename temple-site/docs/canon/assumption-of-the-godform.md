# The Assumption of the Godform

### A Deep Working of the Aeternal Temple of Obsidian Flame

> *The pivot of all theurgy. The technique by which the Magician ceases to invoke
> and begins to be. Written for the College of the Neophyte, with the rite of
> Tahuti (Liber Israfel) as its worked example.*

---

## 0. What It Is, and What It Is Not

The Assumption of the Godform is the deliberate, total identification of the
operator with a divine form, such that the operator's "I" is displaced and the
god speaks, sees, and acts in the first person through the body.

It is **not** psychodrama. Psychodrama keeps the self in the director's chair and
wears the god as a mask to address its own contents. The Assumption removes the
director. The test is simple and merciless: in psychodrama you can, at any moment,
*observe yourself performing*. In the Assumption there is no longer a self
standing apart to observe — there is only the god, and (afterward) the memory of
having been displaced.

This is the operative meaning of the Temple's standing doctrine: **strike the I.**
The whole of the technique below is machinery for the excision of a single letter
from the center of the self. `LXIV → LVX`. Everything that follows is *how*.

---

## I. The Four Prerequisites (do not skip)

The Assumption fails for the unprepared not because the gods are absent but
because the instrument cannot hold the charge. Four capacities must be drilled to
reflex **before** the godform is ever assumed in earnest:

### 1. Stillness of body (Asana)
The form cannot transfer into a fidgeting vessel. Train one posture — the God
position (standing, spine erect, feet together, eyes closed) or the Dragon
(seated on heels) — until you can hold it for thirty minutes without conscious
adjustment. The body must drop out of awareness so the form can occupy it.

### 2. Steadiness of mind (Dharana)
Hold a single image — a triangle, a rose, the bare disk of the moon — for ten
minutes without it warping, sliding, or being interrupted. If you cannot hold a
*static* image, you cannot hold a *living* one. This is the limiting skill; most
failures of Assumption are failures of Dharana wearing a robe.

### 3. The vibrated Word
The divine name is not spoken; it is **vibrated** — driven from the diaphragm so
the breath itself shakes the chest, the skull, the air of the temple. Practice the
single name of the god you will assume until you can feel the vibration leave the
body and continue outward after the breath ends. The Word is the *carrier wave* of
the form.

### 4. Command of the Sphere of Sensation (the aura)
You must be able to feel, and shape, the egg of subtle substance around the body —
to fill it with a chosen color on the exhale, to brighten and dim it at will. The
godform is built first in the Sphere, not in the head. Drill: on each exhale, flood
the aura with one elemental color until the whole egg pulses with it.

> Months 1–2 of practice are *only* these four. The words and gestures of the rite
> can be learned in parallel, but the Assumption proper is not attempted until the
> instrument holds.

## II. The Five Movements of the Assumption

The act itself has a fixed architecture. In Liber Israfel it is spread across
Sections 4–9; here it is named as five movements so it can be drilled as one motion.

### Movement 1 — Construction (the form before you)
Build the godform in the space *in front of you*, at greater-than-human scale, as
an external presence. It is not yet you. Construct it **layer by layer, top to
bottom**, solidifying each layer before adding the next (Liber Israfel §5 gives the
six layers of Tahuti — crown, head, right hand, left hand, headdress, body). Do not
proceed to Movement 2 until the form stands complete, stable, and *seen* — luminous,
dimensional, present enough that the temple is lit by it.

### Movement 2 — Vivification (giving it the senses)
A built image is a statue. Vivify it: see the chest rise and fall with breath, see
the eyes track, let the regalia move and gleam. Charge it with the **vibrated name**
— each vibration should make the form flare and steady. Vivification is what
separates a remembered picture from a thing that can be *inhabited*.

### Movement 3 — The Transfer (the striking of the I)
**The pivot.** Take a single physical pace forward as you exhale, and on that step
**step into the form** — let your head rise into its head, your arms into its arms,
your skin into its blazing skin. The forward step is not symbolic; it is the somatic
trigger that displaces the self. At the instant of merger, *stop narrating.* Do not
think "now I am the god." That thought is the I, still in the chair. Let the next
words out of the body be the god's, in the first person, without a self behind them
checking the work.

> This is where the ego resists — a real, felt clutch of refusal. The resistance is
> the **I** declining to be struck. Breathe through it. Do not force it with effort
> (effort is the I trying harder); release into the form. The Transfer succeeds by
> *yielding*, not by *seizing*.

### Movement 4 — Apotheosis (the Word as law)
From within the form, speak as the god (Israfel §§6–11). The proclamations are not
affirmations to be believed — from inside a genuine Transfer they are simply *true*,
and spoken as fact: *"He is in me, and I in Him… my Word is accomplished every day."*
Touch mouth, heart, tongue as the rite directs; the body is now the god's temple and
every utterance carries divine authority. Here the working proper is done — the
conjuration, the Word, the Light.

### Movement 5 — The Return (decommissioning the form)
**Never omit this.** The form must be set down with the same deliberation it was
taken up. See §IV below. An un-returned godform is the single most common harm in
this work.

## III. The Worked Example — Tahuti (Liber Israfel)

| Movement | Israfel | Act |
|---|---|---|
| Construct | §4–5 | Veil of Paroketh parts; build the six layers — white crown, emerald ibis head, caduceus in the right hand, Rosy Cross in the left, night-blue nemmes, orange-blazing body |
| Vivify | §5 close | The galaxy-eyes track; the caduceus crackles; charge with **TAHUTI** vibrated |
| Transfer | §6 (the step) | One pace forward; the I is struck; the ibis head rises to the ceiling |
| Apotheosis | §6–14 | "I am Yesterday, To-Day, and the Brother of To-Morrow"; the Emerald Tablet; the binding of all spirits |
| Return | §15–21 | Isis/Silence cools the fire; "let it return into the silence of light"; step **backward**; ground; LBRP |

The lunar balance of Isis (§15) is part of the Return, not an ornament: the
solar-Mercurial fire must be cooled to silver before the form is set down, or the
operator carries the heat out of the temple.

## IV. The Return — How to Set Down a God

1. **Cool the current.** Invoke the receptive/silent counter-pole (for Tahuti, Isis
   by the Sign of Silence). Feel the aura's fierce color cool toward silver-blue.
2. **The License to Depart.** Address the form: thank it, release it, and *will* the
   separation — "Depart in peace; let there be peace between us."
3. **Reverse the step.** Take one pace **backward**, the exact reversal of the
   Transfer step. As you do, feel the godform stand forth from you again, external
   once more, and then dissolve.
4. **Reclaim the I — on purpose.** This is the one moment you *deliberately* take the
   self back. State your own name. Feel your ordinary outline return.
5. **Ground.** Three slow breaths into the belly. Feet, hands, the weight of the
   body. Eat something; touch cold water; record the working in the Magickal Diary
   *immediately* — writing is itself a grounding act.
6. **Seal with the LBRP.** The banishing closes the temple and severs any residual
   link to the form.

## V. Failure Modes and Their Remedies

- **Mere visualization (no Transfer).** You saw the god but never stepped in.
  Remedy: strengthen Dharana; make the forward step a hard somatic trigger; stop
  narrating at the merge.
- **Inflation (the I steals the crown).** Afterward you feel grandiose, "chosen,"
  superior. This is the failure of the Return — the I climbed back wearing the
  godform's regalia. Remedy: rigorous Return, hard grounding, the LBRP, and humility
  practices. The god departs; *you* do the dishes.
- **Flux / failure to return fully.** Lingering dissociation, thinning of the self,
  "not all the way back" for hours or days. Remedy: repeat the full Return, eat
  heavily, physical labor, sunlight, sleep; refrain from further god-work until
  stable. Prevention: never skip §IV.
- **The clutch of resistance reads as fear.** Normal at the Transfer. It is the I
  defending itself. Yield, don't shove.

## VI. The Progressive Curriculum

| Period | Work |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | The four prerequisites only. Words/gestures memorized in parallel. No Assumption. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Movement 1 (Construction) and 2 (Vivification) of the chosen form — built *before* you, never yet entered. |
| Month 2 | Attempt the Transfer (Movement 3). Expect ego-resistance. Short holds. Always full Return. |
| Month 3 | Apotheosis carries genuine force — felt vastness, the cooling at the lunar balance, the flame at the Light. |
| Ongoing | The form begins to arise unbidden when its qualities are needed. The Transfer shortens from minutes to a single breath. |

## VII. The Seal

The Assumption is not the wearing of a god. It is the **striking of the I** such
that the Light already present is no longer obstructed by a self standing in front
of it. You do not *add* the god. You *subtract* the I, and what remains was always
LVX.

> `LXIV → strike the I → LVX → 65 → Adonai → the Light.`

*Strike the I, and the Light remains.*

Qabalistic Temple of Obsidian Flame.*
