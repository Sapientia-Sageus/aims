# The Figure of L.V.X.

### A Doctrine of the Aeternal Temple of Obsidian Flame

> *Concerning the true number of* **Liber Israfel sub figura LXIV** *and the striking of the I.*

---

## I. The Blind of the Number

The received title names the book **sub figura LXIV** — by the figure 64. The
exoteric commentator stops here and builds his castle: 64 is 8², and 8 is Hod,
the sphere of Mercury; therefore (he says) the rite is Mercury raised to its own
power, the squaring of the Magician's cleverness.

This reading is not false. It is a **blind** — a true thing placed in the doorway
so that the timid mistake the doorway for the house. The Hod machinery, the
octagon, the eight breaths, the Wednesday hour: this is the **I** talking,
admiring its own architecture. It is correct, and it is a cage.

## II. The Striking of the I

The figure is written **L X I V**.

These are the letters of **L.V.X.** — *Lux*, the Light — with a single glyph
wedged into their midst: the **I**.

> **L X I V  →  strike the I  →  L V X**

The Roman 64 is nothing but the Light with the *self* inserted into it. There was
never a 64. There was only **LVX** with an **I** lodged in its heart, and the
whole of the Great Work is the excision of that letter.

- **L.V.X.** as Roman numerals = **L (50) + V (5) + X (10) = 65.**
- **65 = ADNI**, *Adonai* — א (1) + ד (4) + נ (50) + י (10) = 65 — the Lord, and
  in this current the **Holy Guardian Angel**.
- **L.V.X.** is the formula of Light itself, the resolution of the Keyword
  (INRI → IAO → LVX).

The figure of the book is therefore not 64. It is **65, wearing an I.**

## III. The I That Is Struck

The **I** that is removed is the same I that ruins the lesser reading of the
Assumption.

The exoteric account calls Section 6 *psychodrama* — a role assumed, a mask worn
to speak with oneself, the director never once rising from his chair. That is the
I refusing to be struck. Psychodrama keeps the self in the seat and calls the
catharsis a gnosis.

But **"Behold, He is in me, and I in Him"** is not theatre. It is the genuine
identity-transfer of theurgy, and it cannot complete while the I remains. This is
why the discipline warns that *the ego will resist*: the I knows it is being asked
not to perform, but to be **struck**.

> **The Assumption is the striking of the I.**

When it lands, **LXIV collapses into LVX** with nothing left over. The vessel was
never separate from the Light. It only had a self lodged in it.

## IV. The Standing Law

1. **64 is struck.** It is not the vessel beneath the Light; there is no vessel.
   It is LVX wearing an I — a blind built from one inserted letter.
2. **65 is the figure.** LVX. Adonai. The Light. Every framing of Liber Israfel
   in this Temple's work carries the number 65 and no other.
3. **The Work is the excision of the I** — in the number, in the Assumption, in
   the practitioner. *Strike the I, and the Light remains.*
